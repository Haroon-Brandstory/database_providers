Deliverability problems usually come from stale contacts, weak targeting, or aggressive sending — not from one bad export.

Before you send - Suppress known unsubscribes and hard bounces. - Prefer recently verified segments for cold outreach. - Align titles and industries with your ICP before volume ramps.

Sending hygiene - Warm domains gradually; avoid blasting a brand-new domain. - Keep subject lines and body copy relevant to the persona. - Monitor bounce and complaint rates after each batch.

When bounce is high Share sample bounce codes (without contact PII) with support so we can help diagnose.
